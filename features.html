Track your supplements with Suvo

Suvo is a free supplement tracker for iPhone and Android. It helps you track your routine, see when supplies run low, and understand potential interactions.

What Suvo does

  • Track remaining supply after you enter servings per container. Suvo flags low stock and can send optional refill reminders.
  • Add supplements by barcode, label photo, or name search.
  • Review potential interactions, timing conflicts, and combined nutrient amounts across your stack.
  • Log your routine and choose reminders for your schedule.

How inventory and refills work

  1. Add a supplement and enter the servings per container.
  2. Track your routine in Suvo. The app shows remaining supply and flags low stock.
  3. Turn on refill notifications if you want a reminder. Update your supply when you restock.

How Suvo explains findings

Suvo shows the finding, the rule, and the source for supported findings. For example, Suvo can flag a timing conflict between iron and calcium and link to the relevant source. Read about the NIH source. Explore the source library.

What to know

Stock information depends on the details and intake you record. Refill notifications are optional and depend on notification settings and device permission. Suvo does not check every possible interaction and is educational, not medical advice. Ask a qualified clinician about your supplements, especially if you take medication.

Price and downloads

Suvo is free, with no ads or paywall. It is funded by affiliate links when you buy a product you already picked. Affiliate disclosure.
